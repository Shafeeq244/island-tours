Places worth the trip

Cabbage Beach

A long, wide stretch of soft sand on Paradise Island. Calm enough for swimming, lively enough for jet skis and beach games.

Cable Beach

Resorts, restaurants and a sunset-friendly shoreline west of downtown.

Junkanoo Beach

The easiest beach to reach from the cruise port, with beach bars and water sports.

Fort Fincastle and the Queen's Staircase

Climb 66 steps carved by hand into limestone, then look out over the harbor from the fort.

Fort Charlotte

The island's largest fort, with cannons, dungeons and grassy grounds to wander.

Arawak Cay (the Fish Fry)

A row of colorful local eateries serving fresh conch salad, cracked conch and fried fish. Go hungry.

Nassau Straw Market

Hand-woven baskets, hats and local crafts. Bargaining is part of the fun.

Blue Lagoon Island

A short boat ride to a private island for snorkeling, kayaking and dolphin encounters.

Clifton Heritage National Park

Coastal trails, ruins of a plantation and an underwater sculpture garden for snorkelers.

Things to do

  • Snorkel a reef

    Clear, warm water and plenty of fish close to shore.

  • Sail at sunset

    Catamaran and sailboat cruises leave from the Nassau and Paradise Island harbors.

  • Eat conch every way

    Salad, fritters, chowder and cracked conch. Try them at the Fish Fry.

  • Walk historic Bay Street

    Colonial buildings, shops and the old Pirates of Nassau museum.

  • Taste local rum and chocolate

    Distillery and chocolate factory tours are easy to add to a downtown walk.

  • Meet the animals

    Ardastra Gardens is known for its marching flamingos.

  • See Junkanoo

    The island's street parade of costumes and music runs on Boxing Day and New Year's Day.

  • Take a day trip

    Ferries and flights link Nassau to the Exuma cays and their famous swimming pigs.

One perfect day in Nassau

  1. Morning

    Queen's Staircase and Fort Fincastle

    Go early, before the heat and the crowds.

  2. Midday

    Bay Street and the Straw Market

    Browse, then grab a snack in the shade.

  3. Afternoon

    Junkanoo or Cabbage Beach

    Swim, float and do very little.

  4. Evening

    Dinner at Arawak Cay

    Conch salad made in front of you, with a cold Kalik.

Good to know

Best time to visit
December to April is dry and sunny. Summer is hotter and cheaper, with hurricane season from June to November.
Getting around
Taxis are metered by zone, jitney buses are cheap, and downtown is walkable. Confirm taxi fares before you ride.
Money
The Bahamian dollar is pegged one-to-one with the US dollar, and US dollars are accepted everywhere.
Sun and sea
Bring reef-safe sunscreen, a hat and a refillable water bottle.

Prices, hours and schedules change. Check with each venue before you go.
